연습문제 1.5 — 비선형상미분방정식 (Bernoulli · 치환)
23\(y'+xy=y^{-1},\quad y(0)=3\)베르누이 · 초기값
Step 1 표준형으로 정리 (베르누이 방정식인지 판별)
\[y'+p(x)\,y=g(x)\,y^{a}\qquad p(x)=x,\quad g(x)=1,\quad a=-1\ :\ a\neq 0,\,1\ \text{이므로 비선형 (Bernoulli)}\]
Step 2 치환하여 선형화
\[u=y^{\,1-a}=y^{\,1-(-1)}=y^{2},\qquad u'=2y\,y'=2y\left(-xy+y^{-1}\right)=-2xy^{2}+2=-2xu+2\]
\[\Longrightarrow\quad u'+2x\,u=2\qquad(u\text{에 관한 선형 비제차})\qquad p(x)=2x,\quad r(x)=2\]
Step 3 일반해 구하기
\[h=\int p(x)\,dx=\int 2x\,dx=x^{2}\]
\[u=e^{-h}\left[\int e^{h}\,r\,dx+c\right]=e^{-x^{2}}\left[2\int e^{x^{2}}dx+c\right]\]
\[\quad=e^{-x^{2}}\left[2\int_{0}^{x}e^{t^{2}}dt+c\right]\qquad\left(\textstyle\int e^{x^{2}}dx\text{는 초등함수가 아님 → 정적분, 상수를 }c\text{로 다시 둠}\right)\]
∴ 일반해:\(y^{2}=e^{-x^{2}}\left(2\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}e^{t^{2}}dt+c\right)\)
Step 4 특수해 구하기
초기조건 적용
\[u(0)=y(0)^{2}=9=e^{0}\left(2\cdot 0+c\right)\ \Longrightarrow\ c=9\]
특수해:\(y^{2}=e^{-x^{2}}\left(9+2\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}e^{t^{2}}dt\right)\)
즉\(y=e^{-x^{2}/2}\sqrt{9+2\displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}e^{t^{2}}dt}\)
\(y(0)=3>0\)이므로 양의 근, \(9+2\!\int_{0}^{x}\!e^{t^{2}}dt>0\)인 0을 포함하는 구간
Step 5 검증
\[u=e^{-x^{2}}\Big(9+2\!\int_{0}^{x}\!e^{t^{2}}dt\Big)\ \Longrightarrow\ u'=-2x\,e^{-x^{2}}\Big(9+2\!\int_{0}^{x}\!e^{t^{2}}dt\Big)+e^{-x^{2}}\cdot 2e^{x^{2}}=-2xu+2\]
\[y'=\frac{u'}{2y}=\frac{-2xu+2}{2y}=-\frac{xy^{2}}{y}+\frac{1}{y}=-xy+y^{-1}\ \Longrightarrow\ y'+xy=y^{-1}\quad\checkmark\qquad y(0)=\sqrt{9}=3\quad\checkmark\]
28\(2xy\,y'+(x-1)\,y^{2}=x^{2}e^{x}\quad(y^{2}=z\text{로 놓아라})\)치환 z = y² · 선형
Step 1 치환하여 표준형으로 정리
\[z=y^{2},\qquad z'=2y\,y'\ \Longrightarrow\ x\,z'+(x-1)\,z=x^{2}e^{x}\]
\[z'+\left(1-\frac{1}{x}\right)z=x\,e^{x}\qquad p(x)=1-\frac{1}{x},\quad r(x)=x\,e^{x}\ :\ z\text{에 관한 선형 비제차}\]
Step 2 일반해 구하기
\[h=\int p(x)\,dx=\int\left(1-\frac{1}{x}\right)dx=x-\ln|x|\qquad(x>0\text{ 구간에서})\quad e^{h}=\frac{e^{x}}{x},\qquad e^{-h}=x\,e^{-x}\]
\[z=e^{-h}\left[\int e^{h}\,r\,dx+c\right]=x\,e^{-x}\left[\int\frac{e^{x}}{x}\cdot x\,e^{x}\,dx+c\right]=x\,e^{-x}\left[\int e^{2x}dx+c\right]=x\,e^{-x}\left[\tfrac{1}{2}e^{2x}+c\right]\]
\[\Longrightarrow\quad z=\tfrac{1}{2}x\,e^{x}+c\,x\,e^{-x}\]
∴ 일반해:\(y^{2}=\tfrac{1}{2}x\,e^{x}+c\,x\,e^{-x}=x\left(\tfrac{1}{2}e^{x}+c\,e^{-x}\right)\quad(x\neq 0,\ \text{우변}>0\text{인 구간})\)
Step 3 검증
\[z'=\tfrac{1}{2}e^{x}+\tfrac{1}{2}x\,e^{x}+c\,e^{-x}-c\,x\,e^{-x}\]
\[x\,z'=\tfrac{1}{2}x\,e^{x}+\tfrac{1}{2}x^{2}e^{x}+c\,x\,e^{-x}-c\,x^{2}e^{-x},\qquad (x-1)\,z=\tfrac{1}{2}x^{2}e^{x}-\tfrac{1}{2}x\,e^{x}+c\,x^{2}e^{-x}-c\,x\,e^{-x}\]
\[x\,z'+(x-1)\,z=x^{2}e^{x}\quad\checkmark\]
\[2xy\,y'=x\,z'\ \text{이므로}\ 2xy\,y'+(x-1)y^{2}=x^{2}e^{x}\quad\checkmark\]
연습문제 2.1 — 차수축소법 (한 개의 해 y₁을 알 때)
06\(xy''+2y'+xy=0,\quad y_{1}=\dfrac{\cos x}{x}\)차수축소법
Step 1 표준형으로 정리
\[y''+\frac{2}{x}\,y'+y=0\qquad p(x)=\frac{2}{x},\quad q(x)=1\]
Step 2 차수축소법 적용
\[\int p\,dx=\int\frac{2}{x}\,dx=2\ln|x|,\qquad e^{-\int p\,dx}=e^{-2\ln|x|}=\frac{1}{x^{2}},\qquad y_{1}^{2}=\frac{\cos^{2}x}{x^{2}}\]
\[U=\frac{1}{y_{1}^{2}}\,e^{-\int p\,dx}=\frac{x^{2}}{\cos^{2}x}\cdot\frac{1}{x^{2}}=\sec^{2}x,\qquad u=\int U\,dx=\int\sec^{2}x\,dx=\tan x\]
Step 3 두 번째 해 구하기
\[y_{2}=u\,y_{1}=\tan x\cdot\frac{\cos x}{x}=\frac{\sin x}{x}\]
∴ 기저:\(y_{1}=\dfrac{\cos x}{x},\quad y_{2}=\dfrac{\sin x}{x}\)
일반해:\(y=\dfrac{c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x}{x}\quad(x\neq 0)\)
Step 4 검증
\[y_{2}=\frac{\sin x}{x},\qquad y_{2}'=\frac{x\cos x-\sin x}{x^{2}},\qquad y_{2}''=\frac{-x^{2}\sin x-2x\cos x+2\sin x}{x^{3}}\]
\[x\,y_{2}''+2y_{2}'+x\,y_{2}=\frac{-x^{2}\sin x-2x\cos x+2\sin x}{x^{2}}+\frac{2x\cos x-2\sin x}{x^{2}}+\sin x\]
\[\qquad=-\sin x+\sin x=0\quad\checkmark\]
\[\frac{y_{2}}{y_{1}}=\tan x\neq\text{const}\ \Longrightarrow\ y_{1},\,y_{2}\ \text{1차독립 (기저)}\quad\checkmark\]
09\(x^{2}y''+xy'-4y=0,\quad y_{1}=x^{2}\)차수축소법
Step 1 표준형으로 정리
\[y''+\frac{1}{x}\,y'-\frac{4}{x^{2}}\,y=0\qquad p(x)=\frac{1}{x},\quad q(x)=-\frac{4}{x^{2}}\]
Step 2 차수축소법 적용
\[\int p\,dx=\int\frac{1}{x}\,dx=\ln|x|\qquad(x>0\text{ 구간에서})\quad e^{-\int p\,dx}=e^{-\ln x}=\frac{1}{x},\qquad y_{1}^{2}=x^{4}\]
\[U=\frac{1}{y_{1}^{2}}\,e^{-\int p\,dx}=\frac{1}{x^{4}}\cdot\frac{1}{x}=x^{-5},\qquad u=\int U\,dx=\int x^{-5}dx=-\frac{1}{4}x^{-4}\]
Step 3 두 번째 해 구하기
\[y_{2}=u\,y_{1}=-\frac{1}{4}x^{-4}\cdot x^{2}=-\frac{1}{4}x^{-2}\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{상수배를 떼어}\ y_{2}=x^{-2}=\frac{1}{x^{2}}\]
∴ 기저:\(y_{1}=x^{2},\quad y_{2}=\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}\)
일반해:\(y=c_{1}x^{2}+\dfrac{c_{2}}{x^{2}}\quad(x\neq 0)\)
Step 4 검증
\[y_{2}=x^{-2},\qquad y_{2}'=-2x^{-3},\qquad y_{2}''=6x^{-4}\]
\[x^{2}y_{2}''+x\,y_{2}'-4y_{2}=6x^{-2}-2x^{-2}-4x^{-2}=0\quad\checkmark\]
\[\frac{y_{2}}{y_{1}}=x^{-4}\neq\text{const}\ \Longrightarrow\ y_{1},\,y_{2}\ \text{1차독립 (기저)}\quad\checkmark\]
연습문제 2.2 — 상수계수를 갖는 제차선형상미분방정식 (특성방정식)
4\(y''+4y'+(\pi^{2}+4)\,y=0\)경우 3 · 공액복소근
Step 1 특성방정식
\[y=e^{\lambda x}\ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda^{2}+4\lambda+(\pi^{2}+4)=0\]
Step 2 근의 판별
\[a=4,\ b=\pi^{2}+4:\qquad a^{2}-4b=16-4\pi^{2}-16=-4\pi^{2}\lt 0\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{경우 3 (공액복소근)}\]
\[\lambda=-\tfrac{a}{2}\pm i\,\omega,\qquad \omega=\sqrt{b-\tfrac{a^{2}}{4}}=\sqrt{\pi^{2}+4-4}=\pi\ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda=-2\pm i\pi\]
Step 3 일반해 구하기
∴ 일반해:\(y=e^{-2x}\left(A\cos\pi x+B\sin\pi x\right)\)
Step 4 검증
\[u=A\cos\pi x+B\sin\pi x\ \text{로 놓으면}\ u''=-\pi^{2}u,\qquad y=e^{-2x}u\]
\[y'=e^{-2x}\left(u'-2u\right),\qquad y''=e^{-2x}\left(u''-4u'+4u\right)\]
\[y''+4y'+(\pi^{2}+4)y=e^{-2x}\left[u''-4u'+4u+4u'-8u+(\pi^{2}+4)u\right]=e^{-2x}\left[u''+\pi^{2}u\right]=e^{-2x}\left[-\pi^{2}u+\pi^{2}u\right]=0\quad\checkmark\]
8\(y''+y'+3.25\,y=0\)경우 3 · 공액복소근
Step 1 특성방정식
\[\lambda^{2}+\lambda+3.25=0\]
Step 2 근의 판별
\[a=1,\ b=3.25:\qquad a^{2}-4b=1-13=-12\lt 0\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{경우 3 (공액복소근)}\]
\[\omega=\sqrt{b-\tfrac{a^{2}}{4}}=\sqrt{3.25-0.25}=\sqrt{3}\ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda=-\tfrac{1}{2}\pm i\sqrt{3}\]
Step 3 일반해 구하기
∴ 일반해:\(y=e^{-x/2}\left(A\cos\sqrt{3}\,x+B\sin\sqrt{3}\,x\right)\)
Step 4 검증
\[u=A\cos\sqrt{3}\,x+B\sin\sqrt{3}\,x,\qquad u''=-3u,\qquad y=e^{-x/2}u\]
\[y'=e^{-x/2}\left(u'-\tfrac{1}{2}u\right),\qquad y''=e^{-x/2}\left(u''-u'+\tfrac{1}{4}u\right)\]
\[y''+y'+3.25y=e^{-x/2}\left[u''-u'+\tfrac{1}{4}u+u'-\tfrac{1}{2}u+3.25u\right]=e^{-x/2}\left[u''+3u\right]=e^{-x/2}\left[-3u+3u\right]=0\quad\checkmark\]
14\(y''+2k^{2}y'+k^{4}y=0\)경우 2 · 실이중근
Step 1 특성방정식
\[\lambda^{2}+2k^{2}\lambda+k^{4}=0\]
Step 2 근의 판별
\[a=2k^{2},\ b=k^{4}:\qquad a^{2}-4b=4k^{4}-4k^{4}=0\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{경우 2 (실이중근)}\]
\[\lambda^{2}+2k^{2}\lambda+k^{4}=(\lambda+k^{2})^{2}=0\ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda=-k^{2}\ (\text{이중근})\]
Step 3 일반해 구하기
∴ 일반해:\(y=(c_{1}+c_{2}x)\,e^{-k^{2}x}\)
Step 4 검증
\[y=(c_{1}+c_{2}x)e^{-k^{2}x},\qquad y'=e^{-k^{2}x}\left[c_{2}-k^{2}(c_{1}+c_{2}x)\right]\]
\[y''=e^{-k^{2}x}\left[-k^{2}c_{2}-k^{2}c_{2}+k^{4}(c_{1}+c_{2}x)\right]=e^{-k^{2}x}\left[-2k^{2}c_{2}+k^{4}(c_{1}+c_{2}x)\right]\]
\[y''+2k^{2}y'+k^{4}y=e^{-k^{2}x}\left[-2k^{2}c_{2}+k^{4}(c_{1}+c_{2}x)+2k^{2}c_{2}-2k^{4}(c_{1}+c_{2}x)+k^{4}(c_{1}+c_{2}x)\right]=0\quad\checkmark\]
연습문제 2.3 — 미분연산자 (인수분해)
8\((D^{2}+3I)\,y=0\)연산자 인수분해 · 경우 3
Step 1 특성방정식
\[\lambda^{2}+3=0\]
Step 2 근의 판별
\[a=0,\ b=3:\qquad a^{2}-4b=-12\lt 0\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{경우 3 (공액복소근)},\qquad \lambda=\pm i\sqrt{3}\]
Step 3 일반해 구하기 (연산자 인수분해)
\[D^{2}+3I=(D-i\sqrt{3}\,I)(D+i\sqrt{3}\,I)\qquad(\text{실수 계수의 일차 연산자로는 인수분해되지 않으므로 복소 인수로})\]
\[(D-i\sqrt{3}\,I)\,y_{1}=0\ \Longrightarrow\ y_{1}=e^{i\sqrt{3}x},\qquad (D+i\sqrt{3}\,I)\,y_{2}=0\ \Longrightarrow\ y_{2}=e^{-i\sqrt{3}x}\]
\[\text{두 인수는 교환되므로}\ (D^{2}+3I)y_{1}=(D+i\sqrt{3}I)(D-i\sqrt{3}I)y_{1}=0,\quad (D^{2}+3I)y_{2}=0\]
\[\cos\sqrt{3}\,x=\tfrac{1}{2}(y_{1}+y_{2}),\qquad \sin\sqrt{3}\,x=\tfrac{1}{2i}(y_{1}-y_{2})\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{실수 기저}\ \cos\sqrt{3}\,x,\ \sin\sqrt{3}\,x\]
∴ 일반해:\(y=A\cos\sqrt{3}\,x+B\sin\sqrt{3}\,x\)
Step 4 검증
\[y'=-\sqrt{3}A\sin\sqrt{3}\,x+\sqrt{3}B\cos\sqrt{3}\,x,\qquad y''=-3A\cos\sqrt{3}\,x-3B\sin\sqrt{3}\,x=-3y\]
\[(D^{2}+3I)\,y=y''+3y=-3y+3y=0\quad\checkmark\]
